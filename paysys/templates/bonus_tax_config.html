📌 How Bonus Tax Works

  • Step 1: Calculate Annual Basic Salary (Base Salary × 12)
  • Step 2: Calculate Threshold Amount = Annual Basic Salary × Threshold %
  • Step 3: If Bonus <= Threshold Amount → Bonus taxed at Flat Rate
  • Step 4: If Bonus > Threshold Amount → Threshold portion taxed at Flat Rate, Excess added to salary and taxed at graduated rates
  • Example: Annual Basic = GHS 120,000, Threshold = 15% → GHS 18,000. Bonus of GHS 20,000 → GHS 18,000 @ 5%, GHS 2,000 added to salary for graduated tax
