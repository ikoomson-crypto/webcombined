📖 How Bonus Tax Works

  • Step 2: Calculate Threshold Amount = Annual Basic Salary × {{ current_config.bonus_threshold_percentage if current_config else 15 }}%
  • Step 3: If Bonus <= Threshold Amount → Bonus taxed at {{ current_config.bonus_tax_rate if current_config else 5 }}% flat rate
  • Step 4: If Bonus > Threshold Amount → Threshold portion taxed at {{ current_config.bonus_tax_rate if current_config else 5 }}%, Excess added to salary for graduated tax
